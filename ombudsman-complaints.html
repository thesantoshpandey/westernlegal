Common questions

FAQs.

Is it worth paying a solicitor when the ombudsman is free?

The scheme is free; winning is not automatic. Decisions turn on the written file, and most complainants write grievances where adjudicators need chronology, rule, breach, loss, remedy. You pay for the submission being built like a case. Where the amount at stake is small, the £150 triage will tell you honestly that it is not worth going further.

Which ombudsman do I need?

Financial services, including banks, insurers and lenders, go to the Financial Ombudsman Service. Estate and letting agents go to the Property Ombudsman or PRS. Energy and rail have their own schemes, and many retail sectors have ADR bodies. The triage identifies the right scheme and the deadline, which is commonly six months from the final response.

What can the ombudsman actually award?

Compensation for financial loss, distress and inconvenience awards, and directions to put things right. Awards are enforceable once accepted. The submission always specifies the remedy, because schemes rarely award what nobody asked for.

My complaint was rejected. Is that the end?

Not necessarily. A provisional decision can be challenged with further submissions, and a final decision you reject leaves court routes open. The triage will say plainly which category yours is in.
