Why this practice

Built for firms that need documents to hold up.

CROSS CUTTING

Good faith, no foreseeable harm

PRIN 2A.2 requires a firm to act in good faith towards retail customers, avoid causing them foreseeable harm, and enable and support them to pursue their financial objectives. PRIN 2A.1.10G says the outcome rules set out key obligations on product governance, price and value, consumer understanding and supporting consumers.

GOVERNANCE

Policies the board can stand behind

Where SYSC 6 applies, SYSC 6.1.1R requires adequate policies and procedures sufficient to ensure compliance with the regulatory system. PRIN 2A.8.1R requires the Consumer Duty to be reflected in strategy, governance, leadership and people policies, including incentives at all levels.

SM&CR

Responsibilities on paper

An application to approve a senior manager must include a statement of responsibilities (s.60(2A) FSMA). A firm must take reasonable care that staff performing certification functions hold a valid certificate (s.63E), and SYSC 25.2.1R requires a management responsibilities map in the firms SYSC 25.1.1R brings within that chapter: SMCR banks, Solvency II insurers and enhanced scope firms. The FCA changed several rules in PS26/6, most from 24 April 2026, so we work to the rules in force on the day.

Common questions

Consumer Duty and compliance FAQs.

Who does the Consumer Duty apply to?

PRIN 2A.1.3G says it applies in relation to a firm’s retail market business, and where a firm communicates or approves financial promotions likely to be received by retail customers. To the extent it applies, Principles 6 and 7 do not.

What must the annual board review cover?

Under PRIN 2A.8.4R the governing body must, at least annually, review and approve the report on the outcomes retail customers are receiving, confirm whether it is satisfied the firm is complying with the Consumer Duty, and assess whether its future strategy is consistent with it. PRIN 2A.8.5R requires it to agree any actions needed.

What does acting in good faith mean?

PRIN 2A.2.2R defines it as a standard of conduct characterised by honesty, fair and open dealing and acting consistently with the reasonable expectations of retail customers.

Do individuals have duties under the Consumer Duty too?

Yes. Rule 6 of the individual conduct rules in COCON 2.1 requires those the rules apply to to act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers, alongside the rule to act with integrity.

What is a statement of responsibilities?

It is the statement that must accompany an application to approve a person to perform a designated senior management function, setting out the aspects of the firm’s affairs that person will be responsible for managing (s.60(2A) and (2B) FSMA).

Is the senior managers regime changing?

Yes. The FCA made changes in policy statement PS26/6, most taking effect on 24 April 2026, and has said it expects to consult on a second phase if the Treasury’s proposed legislative changes are made. We check the current rules before every piece of work.

Can you act as our compliance officer?

No. We draft, review and advise in writing. We do not perform controlled functions or compliance monitoring for firms.
