THRESHOLD CONDITIONS

The test the FCA applies

Under s.55B(3) FSMA the FCA must ensure you will satisfy, and continue to satisfy, the threshold conditions. For a firm the PRA does not regulate they are location of offices, effective supervision, appropriate resources, suitability and business model (Schedule 6, paragraphs 2B to 2F). Every document we draft is mapped to them.

COMPLETE FIRST

The six month clock

Section 55V gives the FCA six months from receipt of a completed application and up to twelve months for an incomplete one, and s.55U(5) lets it ask for further information at any point before it decides. A complete, consistent file is the fastest route.

CLEAN LINES

Legal work, not a regulatory host

Advice comes from a solicitor of England and Wales. Western Legal is not an FCA authorised firm: we draft and review, and you submit. If a decision is challenged, that work goes to instructed counsel or an authorised firm, with the file prepared.

Common questions

FCA authorisation FAQs.

How long does the FCA take to decide?

Section 55V of FSMA requires the FCA to determine a complete application within six months of receiving it. An incomplete application must be determined within twelve months of receipt. For an application that relates only to insurance distribution activity, the six months becomes three months (s.55V(9)).

What are the threshold conditions?

They are the minimum conditions in Schedule 6 to FSMA. For a firm whose activities do not include a PRA regulated activity they are location of offices, effective supervision, appropriate resources, suitability and business model, in paragraphs 2B to 2F. COND 2.7.8G lists what the FCA expects a firm to consider about its business model, including the assumptions behind it, its viability and the needs of and risks to consumers.

Is it an offence to carry on a regulated activity without permission?

Yes. Section 19 of FSMA prohibits carrying on a regulated activity in the UK unless you are an authorised or exempt person, and s.23 makes a breach an offence punishable on indictment by up to two years’ imprisonment, a fine or both. That is why the perimeter question comes first.

What does the FCA look at under appropriate resources?

Paragraph 2D of Schedule 6 requires your resources to be appropriate to the activities, taking into account the nature and scale of the business, the risks to the continuity of your services and your group. COND 2.4.2G says the FCA reads appropriate as sufficient in quantity, quality and availability, covering financial and non financial resources such as capital, people and the means to manage risk.

What happens if the FCA proposes to refuse?

It must first give you a warning notice and, if it then decides to refuse, a decision notice (s.55X FSMA). We draft your written representations in response. Any onward challenge goes to instructed counsel or an authorised firm, with the file prepared.

Do you submit the application for us?

No. You are the applicant and you submit it. We prepare and review the documents, draft answers to the case officer’s questions with you in writing, and keep the file consistent. Western Legal is not authorised by the FCA.
