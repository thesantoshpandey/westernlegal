Registration still applies

Regulation 56 of the Money Laundering Regulations 2017 prohibits acting as a cryptoasset exchange provider or custodian wallet provider unless you are on the register or a transitional provision applies. The FCA must refuse registration unless the firm and its officers, managers and beneficial owners are fit and proper (regulation 58A).

THE WINDOW

Apply by 28 February 2027

The FCA says firms that apply in the application period can rely on a saving provision allowing them to continue to provide cryptoasset services, and must notify it that they are doing so. It has also said it will not expedite its assessment of an application to compensate for late submission.

25 OCTOBER 2027

A new perimeter

The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026 come fully into force on 25 October 2027 (regulation 1(2)). The FCA lists the activities as issuing qualifying stablecoins, operating cryptoasset trading platforms, dealing and arranging deals, safeguarding, and arranging cryptoasset staking.

Common questions

Cryptoasset registration FAQs.

Does our MLR registration convert into FCA authorisation?

No. The FCA has said that firms registered with it under the Money Laundering Regulations should note there will be no automatic conversion. A registered firm that falls within the new regime needs authorisation under FSMA.

When is the application window?

The FCA says the application period started on 30 September 2026 and will close on 28 February 2027.

When does the new regime start?

Regulation 1(2) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026 brings them into force on 25 October 2027, the full commencement day.

How long does MLR registration take?

For a cryptoasset exchange provider or custodian wallet provider, regulation 59(3A) gives the FCA three months from receipt of the application, or of any further information it asks for, to register you or tell you it is minded to refuse.

What does the FCA check on registration?

Under regulation 58A the FCA must refuse registration unless the applicant and its officers, managers and beneficial owners are fit and proper. It considers compliance history, the risk that the business may be used for money laundering or terrorist financing, and skills, experience and probity.

Can you approve our cryptoasset promotions?

No. Approval for the purposes of s.21 FSMA can only be given by an authorised person, and under s.55NA only with FCA permission to approve. Western Legal is not authorised by the FCA. We review promotions and advise in writing.
