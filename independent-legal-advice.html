Common questions

ILA FAQs.

What is an ILA certificate and why does my lender want one?

When you give a personal guarantee or waive rights as an occupier, the lender needs to know you understood what you signed, otherwise the document is vulnerable to being set aside later. A solicitor's certificate of independent legal advice is how lenders close that risk, and most will not complete without it.

Can the advice really be given remotely?

Yes, by video call, with identity verified and the certificate signed electronically or in wet ink and posted, depending on what your lender requires. We confirm the lender's specific certificate requirements before the call so there is only one round.

How fast can you do it? My completion is this week.

Standard turnaround is 48 hours from payment and papers. Same-day appointments are available at the urgent rate, tell us the completion date in your enquiry and it will be worked to.

Do you advise me whether to sign the guarantee?

The advice covers the nature and effect of the document, the worst-case exposure, and the questions to ask before signing. The commercial decision stays yours, but you make it knowing exactly what the document does.

My spouse/partner has been asked for ILA on the same deal. Can you advise both of us?

Usually not, independence is the point, and lenders typically require separately advised parties to have separate advisers. We can advise one of you and point the other to an appropriate adviser.
