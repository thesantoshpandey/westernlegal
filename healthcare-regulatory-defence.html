Common questions

FAQs.

Should I just explain everything to the regulator myself?

Early, unadvised accounts are the most common self-inflicted wound in regulatory cases: they fix your version before you have seen the evidence, and inconsistencies later are treated as dishonesty. Take advice before you respond, even the acknowledgment matters.

Does my indemnity provider cover this?

Often, in part, check your policy and your defence organisation cover first, and say what you have in the enquiry. This practice can work alongside or instead of that cover, and will tell you plainly if you are already covered for something you would otherwise pay for.

What is 'insight' and why does everyone keep saying it?

Regulators forgive clinical error far more readily than they forgive its denial. A response that demonstrates reflection, remediation and understanding of what went wrong is frequently the difference between advice, restrictions and erasure, and it must be genuine, which is why it is drafted with you, not for you.

Can this be handled discreetly?

Yes, everything is remote, privileged and confidential by default, and client names are never published by this practice.
