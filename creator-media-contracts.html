Common questions

FAQs.

The brand sent their 'standard terms'. Aren't those non-negotiable?

Almost never, 'standard' means 'drafted for the brand'. The usual wins on review: limiting perpetual usage rights, narrowing exclusivity to the actual category and term, and payment terms that don't leave you financing a multinational for 90 days.

What's the most expensive clause creators miss?

Usage rights. A flat-fee post that quietly licenses the brand to run your face in paid ads, worldwide, forever, is the difference between a £2,000 deal and a £20,000 one. It is one clause, and it is negotiable.

Why does it matter that you run an audience yourself?

Because the advice is calibrated to how these deals actually work, briefs, whitelisting, boosting, exclusivity windows, not translated from a textbook. The practice runs a six-figure-follower legal media brand and manages talent; this is home ground.

Do you act for brands and agencies too?

Yes, campaign agreements, talent contracts and UGC terms from the commissioning side, at the same schedule.
