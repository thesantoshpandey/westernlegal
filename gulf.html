English law is the governing law of Gulf trade

Cross border contracts across the Gulf are routinely written under English law, because it is predictable, well documented and understood by counterparties on both sides.

We draft and review distribution, agency, supply, licensing, SaaS and services agreements under English law for companies in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman and Jordan.

Arbitration is the enforcement route that works

An English court judgment is not straightforwardly enforceable across much of the region. An arbitral award is, because Gulf states are parties to the New York Convention.

That makes the dispute clause the most valuable clause in your contract. We draft it around where your counterparty holds assets, not as boilerplate.

UK trademarks and UK market entry

Gulf brands expanding into the United Kingdom need a UK registration. Filing, prosecution and opposition defence are handled here at fixed fees.

For companies setting up in the UK we deliver the structure, the English law commercial terms, trademark protection and a compliance map as one package.

Common questions

FAQs.

Do you advise on UAE or Saudi law?

No. We advise on English law and on UK and EU trademark procedure. Local law questions are referred to correspondent counsel in the relevant jurisdiction, with the cost agreed with you first.

Can you accept documents in Arabic?

Yes, send them in whichever language you hold them. Where a certified translation is needed we arrange it and confirm the cost before it is incurred. The advice itself is delivered in English.

Will an English law contract be enforceable here?

An arbitral award under an English law contract is enforceable across the Gulf under the New York Convention. A court judgment is a harder route, which is why the dispute clause matters more than most clients expect.

How are fees charged?

Fixed fees in USD agreed in writing before work begins, valid for 30 days. Official fees are passed through at cost. No VAT is charged.
