Common questions

Trademark FAQs.

What does the £555 UK fee include?

Everything for one class: the free conflict search, drafting the specification, filing at the UKIPO within 48 hours of instruction, responding to standard examination objections, and your registration certificate, with the UKIPO official fee included. No VAT, no hidden extras.

Can you file in the EU and India as well?

Yes. EU trademarks are filed via our EU correspondent agent (from €600 plus the EUIPO fee), and Indian marks are filed directly by a qualified Indian Advocate at £120 per class, all official fees included, one practice across all three registers.

What happens if my application is opposed?

You are represented by a solicitor with a proven Tribunal record: a contested UKIPO opposition defended and won outright, with costs awarded, against specialist national IP firm Appleyard Lees (Decision O/1132/25). TM8 counterstatements, evidence rounds, invalidation and revocation are all handled in-house.

Why use a solicitor instead of a filing website?

Most filing problems are specification problems, the goods and services were wrong on day one. A solicitor brings legal privilege, judgement on class strategy, and the ability to defend the mark when it is challenged. The contested matters are where the difference shows, and they are cheapest to prevent at filing.

I own a 'Brexit clone' UK registration. Should I be worried?

Possibly. Since 1 January 2026, cloned UK registrations are exposed to non-use cancellation unless there has been genuine use in the UK. If your use has all been in the EU, your UK right may now be vulnerable, read our guide or send the registration number for a fixed-fee portfolio check.
