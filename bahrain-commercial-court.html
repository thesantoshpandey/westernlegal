Who it is for

Who the BICC is for.

The Bahrain International Commercial Court was established by Legislative Decree No. 9 of 2024, issued on 27 August 2024, and opened on 5 November 2025.

FOREIGN PARTIES

Foreign companies with a Bahrain counterparty

International commercial disputes where both sides agree to the Court.

BAHRAIN PARTIES

Bahrain companies with a foreign counterparty

A neutral forum for a cross border contract, with appeals that can go to Singapore.

Jurisdiction

What the BICC hears.

International disputes

A dispute is international if a party, the place where substantial obligations are performed, or the place most closely connected with the dispute is outside Bahrain. Article 10(a) of Legislative Decree No. 9 of 2024.

Commercial disputes

A dispute is commercial whether it is contractual or not. Article 10(a).

Express consent

The parties must expressly consent to the Court's jurisdiction. Where a claim is filed without a prior agreement, the other party must expressly show its consent. Article 10.

Appeals

Appeals against judgments on the merits issued in English go to the judicial authority named in an international agreement on appeals, which is the International Committee of the Singapore International Commercial Court, unless the parties agreed that the Court's own Appellate Tribunal will hear the appeal. Article 17.

What falls outside

Without express consent the Court has no jurisdiction, and a dispute that is not international, as Article 10 defines it, is outside it. We check this first, in the written claim review, before anything is filed.

Rights of audience

Who acts for you before the BICC.

Under Article 18(b) of Legislative Decree No. 9 of 2024, non Bahraini lawyers may represent parties in disputes conducted in a language other than Arabic. In disputes conducted in Arabic, a non Bahraini lawyer represents a party jointly with a Bahraini lawyer licensed to appear before the Court of Cassation (Article 18(a)).

Common questions

BICC FAQs.

Can a foreign lawyer appear before the BICC?

Yes, in disputes conducted in a language other than Arabic: Article 18(b) of Legislative Decree No. 9 of 2024. In disputes conducted in Arabic, a non Bahraini lawyer acts jointly with a Bahraini lawyer licensed to appear before the Court of Cassation (Article 18(a)).

Do both parties have to agree to the BICC?

Yes. Article 10 requires the parties to consent expressly to the Court's jurisdiction. Where a claim is filed without a prior agreement, the other party must expressly show its consent.

Where do appeals from the BICC go?

Under Article 17, appeals against judgments on the merits issued in English go to the judicial authority named in an international agreement on appeals, which is the International Committee of the Singapore International Commercial Court, unless the parties agreed that the Court's own Appellate Tribunal will hear them.
