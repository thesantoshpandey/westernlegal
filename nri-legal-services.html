The corridor problem, solved structurally.

No relay, no leakage

Normally an NRI matter means a UK adviser relaying to an Indian firm they found online. Here both ends sit inside one privileged relationship with one practitioner answerable to the SRA for the whole of it.

Common questions

NRI FAQs.

Can a UK solicitor really handle my property matter in India?

This one can, because he is also an enrolled Indian Advocate with a Delhi office (P&Y Law Offices, Defence Colony). You instruct in the UK, under a UK-regulated relationship, and the Indian work is executed in India by the same practitioner's own practice, not passed to a stranger.

What NRI matters do you take on?

Indian property disputes and documentation, recovery of money owed in India, powers of attorney drafted and prepared for use in India, family settlements and partition matters, succession certificates, and cross-border estate planning. Court appearances in India are conducted through the Delhi office.

How do powers of attorney for India work from the UK?

The POA is drafted to Indian requirements, executed by you in the UK, and prepared for attestation and use in India, with the exact attestation route confirmed for your state and transaction before you sign anything.

Can you write a will covering assets in both countries?

Yes, coordinated UK and Indian wills drafted so they work together rather than revoking each other, which is the classic cross-border mistake. Note: will drafting is in scope; administering probate is not, and you will be told plainly where that line sits.

I'm in the UK and being chased about a matter in India. Where do I even start?

Send the papers. A fixed-fee triage (£150, credited against any engagement) gets you a written view on what the matter is, what it needs, and what it will cost, in plain English, from someone qualified on both sides.
