Common questions

FAQs.

Will a solicitor's letter really make a difference?

Debtors and opponents triage correspondence: an email from you is a negotiation, a protocol-compliant letter from a solicitor is a costs risk. Most straightforward debts resolve at the letter or statutory-demand stage, which is why those stages are priced to be used early.

What if they still don't pay?

The next steps, county court claim, statutory demand follow-through, enforcement, are mapped in the triage with realistic costs so you can make a commercial decision, not an emotional one. Proceedings are prepared here, with hearings briefed to counsel where advocacy is needed.

Can you defend as well as chase?

Yes. Responding to letters of claim, negotiating settlements and preparing defences is the same skill set from the other chair.

Is small-debt recovery worth legal fees?

At £345 for the letter, the break-even is low, and a pattern of unpaid invoices usually reflects contract terms that need fixing, which is a one-time repair on the contracts page.
