REACH

Article 3(2) in plain terms

Offering goods or services to people in the EU, paid or free, or monitoring their behaviour online, brings you within the EU GDPR wherever you are. A UK company selling to French customers is inside. So is a US company with EU subscribers.

TWO LAWS

UK and EU are no longer one

Since 2021 the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR are separate regimes with separate regulators, separate representative requirements and diverging transfer rules. A business serving both markets needs documents that satisfy both, not one set with the name changed.

HONEST SCOPE

What we do and do not do

We advise on EU GDPR compliance from London. We do not sit as your EU representative, because that must be an entity established in a member state, and we do not advise on member state law. Both are arranged through the correspondent network when needed.

Common questions

EU GDPR FAQs.

Does the EU GDPR apply to a UK company?

Yes, if it offers goods or services to individuals in the EU or monitors their behaviour, under Article 3(2). Brexit removed the UK from the EU; it did not remove EU customers from the EU GDPR.

Do we need an EU representative?

If Article 3(2) applies and you have no establishment in the EU, Article 27 requires a representative in a member state where your data subjects are, unless an exemption applies. We assess that as part of the applicability review and introduce a representative where one is needed.

Can Western Legal be our EU representative?

No. The EU representative must be established in an EU member state. We advise on the appointment, review the terms and introduce a representative through our correspondent network.

We already comply with the UK GDPR. Is that enough for the EU?

Mostly, not entirely. Representative requirements, transfer mechanisms, the lead supervisory authority and some member state rules differ. The dual compliance pack closes the gaps from one document set.

How are transfers from the EU to the UK handled?

The EU adequacy decision for the UK covers transfers from the EU to the UK; transfers onward from the UK, or from the EU to the US or elsewhere, need a mechanism such as standard contractual clauses with a transfer risk assessment. We document the right one for your flows.

Is this advice on French, German or Irish law?

No. It is advice on the EU Regulation as it applies to a business outside the EU. Member state supplementary law and any dealings with a national regulator are handled through correspondent counsel in that state.
