Common questions

FAQs.

Are you Irish solicitors?

No. Santosh Pandey is a Solicitor of England and Wales, not admitted in Ireland, and we advise on English law only. For Irish law you need an Irish solicitor.

My EU trademark predates 2021. Am I covered in the UK?

If it was registered before 1 January 2021, a comparable UK right was created automatically at the end of the transition period. We confirm your exact position as part of any instruction.

How are fees charged?

Fixed fees in EUR agreed in writing before work begins, official fees at cost, quotes valid for 30 days.
