Who it is for

Who the QFC Court is for.

The Civil and Commercial Court of the Qatar Financial Centre is a Qatari court with an international nature (Article 3.1 of its Rules and Procedures). With the QFC Regulatory Tribunal it makes up the Qatar International Court and Dispute Resolution Centre (QICDRC).

QFC FIRMS

Companies established in the QFC

More than 4,700 firms were registered in the Qatar Financial Centre by the end of June 2026, banks, funds and consultancies among them. Disputes between them, and with QFC bodies, come to this Court.

COUNTERPARTIES

Contractors and counterparties

Contractors, employees, Qatar residents and companies outside the QFC in a dispute with a QFC company, unless the contract sends the dispute somewhere else.

ARBITRATION

Parties whose arbitration names the Court

Where the parties agree, the Court is the competent court for their arbitration under Qatar's Arbitration Law, and its enforcement judge enforces the award.

English law work for Gulf companies outside court is on the Gulf page.

Jurisdiction

What the Court hears, and what it does not.

QFC party disputes

Civil and commercial disputes between entities established in the QFC, with QFC bodies, with their contractors and employees, and between a QFC entity and a Qatar resident or a company outside the QFC, unless the parties agree otherwise. Article 8 of the QFC Law (Law No. 7 of 2005); Article 9.1 of the Rules and Procedures.

Qatar Free Zones disputes

Disputes between companies registered in the Qatar Free Zones, between them and the Free Zones Authority, and between them and Qatar residents or companies outside the Free Zones, unless the parties agree to settle by alternative means. Article 44 of Law No. 34 of 2005 on Free Zones, as amended by Law No. 15 of 2021.

Arbitration supervision

By agreement of the parties, the First Instance Circuit is the Competent Court, and its enforcement judge the Competent Judge, under Qatar's Law No. 2 of 2017 on Arbitration in Civil and Commercial Matters (Article 1): appointing arbitrators, applications to set an award aside, and enforcement.

Regulatory Tribunal appeals

The Appeal Circuit hears appeals against judgments of the QFC Regulatory Tribunal, as well as appeals from the First Instance Circuit. Article 9.2 of the Rules and Procedures.

What it does not hear

Two parties with no QFC connection cannot bring their dispute to the Court by choosing it in their contract. In University of Cambridge v The Holding WLL [2025] QIC (A) 6, decided on 29 April 2025, the Appellate Division held that the Court has the jurisdiction set out in Article 8 of the QFC Law and other primary legislation, and that its Rules cannot extend that to a general opt in (paragraph 58). Where the Court finds it has no jurisdiction, it refers the case to the competent court in Qatar (Article 9.4). We check jurisdiction first, in the written claim review, before anything is filed.

Rights of audience

Who acts for you before the Court.

Under Article 30.1 of the Rules and Procedures before the Civil and Commercial Court of the Qatar Financial Centre, the President or the Judge presiding over the Circuit may decide who has rights of audience in a case. Where no direction has been made, rights of audience belong to any qualified lawyer “entitled to appear before the superior courts” of Qatar or of any other jurisdiction.

Common questions

QFC Court FAQs.

Can a solicitor of England and Wales appear before the QFC Court?

Yes, unless the Court directs otherwise. Article 30.1 of the Rules and Procedures gives rights of audience to any qualified lawyer entitled to appear before the superior courts of Qatar or of any other jurisdiction, and the President or the presiding Judge may direct who appears in a particular case. Santosh Pandey, Solicitor of England and Wales, acts.

Can two companies outside the QFC choose the QFC Court in their contract?

No. In University of Cambridge v The Holding WLL [2025] QIC (A) 6 the Appellate Division held that the Court has only the jurisdiction given by Article 8 of the QFC Law and other primary legislation, and that its Rules cannot create a general opt in.

Does the QFC Court hear Qatar Free Zones disputes?

Yes. Article 44 of Law No. 34 of 2005 on Free Zones, as amended by Law No. 15 of 2021, gives the Court jurisdiction over disputes between companies registered in the Free Zones, with the Free Zones Authority, and with Qatar residents or companies outside the Free Zones, unless the parties agree to settle by alternative means.

Can the QFC Court supervise our arbitration?

Yes, if the parties agree. Under Article 1 of Qatar's Law No. 2 of 2017 on Arbitration in Civil and Commercial Matters, the Competent Court can be the First Instance Circuit of the QFC Court, and the Competent Judge its enforcement judge, by agreement of the parties.
