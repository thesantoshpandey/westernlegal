Common questions

FAQs.

Can you guarantee content comes down?

No honest lawyer can, platforms and publishers make their own decisions. What a properly framed legal letter does is move your complaint from the ignore pile to the legal team's desk with the correct legal hooks attached, which is where most removals actually happen.

What is the 'Streisand risk' you mention?

Escalating can amplify: a heavy-handed letter that becomes a story is worse than the original post. Part of the triage is an honest view on whether legal pressure helps your situation or feeds it, and you will get that view even when it means less work for this practice.

Someone is impersonating me or my business online.

Impersonation cases move fastest, because platforms have clear policies against them. Send the links and your proof of identity or trademark; a registered mark makes this dramatically easier, see the trademarks page.

Is this confidential? The matter is sensitive.

Entirely. Instructions to a solicitor are privileged, everything is handled remotely, and client names are never published.
