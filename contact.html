Common questions

FAQs.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within 24 hours of your enquiry, in writing. That is a promise, not an aspiration, and it applies to every enquiry received.

Will you tell me the price before I commit?

Yes. You get a fixed fee, the scope it covers and the timescale, in writing, before any payment and before any work begins. The fee quoted is the fee paid.

Do I need to come to a meeting?

No. Everything is handled in writing and delivered remotely across England and Wales, the EU and internationally. Where a matter includes a remote hearing, that is stated in the fee tier.

Is my enquiry confidential?

Yes. Enquiries are treated as confidential from the first message, whether or not you go on to instruct.

What should I include in the first message?

The documents, the deadline if there is one, and a short chronology. That is usually enough for a written view and a fixed quote without further back and forth.

What language do you work in?

Enquiries are welcome in many languages including Arabic, Persian, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Dutch and Turkish. All advice, drafting and correspondence is delivered in English, because the work is English law. Certified translation and interpreters are arranged where needed, with the cost agreed before it is incurred.

Do you act for clients outside the UK?

Yes. We act for clients across Europe, the Gulf, North America and Asia on English law matters and on UK and EU trademark procedure. Everything is handled in writing and delivered remotely, so your location makes no difference to the service or the timescale.
