Common questions

FAQs.

Why fixed fees for HR processes?

Because the incentive should be to finish your problem, not to bill it. A disciplinary supported at £495 costs less than one hour of a City employment partner, and an unfair-dismissal claim costs multiples of the whole schedule.

Our contracts were downloaded years ago. Does it matter?

Usually the gaps only show when someone leaves badly: no enforceable restrictive covenants, no IP assignment, notice terms that don't say what you think. A contract review is the cheapest audit your business will buy this year.

Do you deal with settlement agreements for exits?

Yes, drafting on the employer side at £495, and the protected-conversation approach advised before you have it. See the settlement agreements page.

What does the retainer actually get us?

A solicitor who already knows your business, answering the 20-minute questions that prevent the £20,000 problems, plus the document allowance and priority turnaround set out in the retainer letter.
